\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
GoF \doxylink{class_subject}{Subject} role: something that can be observed. 

\doxylink{class_subject}{Subject} stores non-\/owning raw pointers to its observers. Whoever created an \doxylink{class_observer}{Observer} is responsible for its lifetime and must \doxylink{class_subject_a752a7596d52ab986539232f8377ca92c}{detach()} it before it is destroyed
