\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
GoF Composite role, and simultaneously both \doxylink{class_subject}{Subject} and \doxylink{class_observer}{Observer}. 

\doxylink{class_event_group}{Event\+Group} plays three roles for three different reasons:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Composite: it owns a list of child Event\+Component\texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*} and implements open/\+close/\+report\+Status/\+get\+Capacity recursively over that tree.
\item \doxylink{class_subject}{Subject}: it can have its own observers attached to it, and \doxylink{class_subject_ab5b0a79cd1214e08f11dbe18611e8f8c}{notify()} pushes a \doxylink{struct_notice}{Notice} down to them.
\item \doxylink{class_observer}{Observer}: it can itself be \doxylink{class_subject_a0884bfe374d352c6488a1e40c84246c8}{attach()}ed to a parent \doxylink{class_event_group}{Event\+Group} or to \doxylink{class_event_control}{Event\+Control}, so that a notice issued above cascades down to it and, via its own \doxylink{class_subject_ab5b0a79cd1214e08f11dbe18611e8f8c}{notify()}, further down again. 
\end{DoxyItemize}
